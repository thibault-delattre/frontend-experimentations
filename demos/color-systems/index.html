Modern Colour

OKLCH, relative colour syntax, color-mix(), interpolation spaces, wide gamut and light-dark(). Move the hue slider — the entire page is derived from one number.

1 · A perceptual ramp

Twelve steps of equal lightness spacing at constant hue. In HSL the same sweep would look bright in yellow and muddy in blue; in OKLCH each step is the same perceived jump, which is what makes generated palettes usable without hand-tuning.

2 · Relative colour syntax

oklch(from var(--base) calc(l * 0.55) c h) — take a colour apart, do maths on its channels, put it back. Hover states, disabled states and borders all derive from a single token, so a runtime brand colour still produces a coherent set.

4 · Wide gamut

On a P3 display the right swatch is visibly more saturated. On sRGB they are identical, because the fallback declaration is the same colour.

rgb(255 0 80)
color(display-p3 1 0 .31)

Rules of thumb

  • Store tokens as OKLCH triples, not hex. Derivation is then arithmetic.
  • Chroma above ~0.25 is out of sRGB for most hues; browsers gamut-map it, so two "different" high-chroma values can render identically.
  • Contrast is still a WCAG calculation on relative luminance — equal OKLCH lightness does not guarantee an equal contrast ratio against a given background. Check, or use contrast-color().
  • Set color-scheme or form controls and scrollbars stay light.
