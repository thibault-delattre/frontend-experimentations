Toasts & Notifications

Ephemeral feedback is a timing and accessibility problem disguised as a card. These examples queue, pause, announce, dismiss, and preserve actionable messages.

1 · A queue, not a pile

Newest items arrive nearest the viewport edge; the stack caps at four, and duplicate events update an existing toast instead of creating noise.

2 · Timers respect attention

Hover or focus a toast and both its visual countdown and removal timer pause. Errors remain until dismissed because destructive information must not evaporate.

3 · Swipe with commitment

Drag a toast horizontally. It dismisses only beyond 35% width or at flick velocity; otherwise it springs back. Mouse, touch, keyboard, and close buttons all work.
