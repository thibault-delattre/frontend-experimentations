03 · masked media

Windows move slower than their contents

Each frame reveals with clip-path while the oversized artwork travels in the opposite direction. ScrollTrigger owns local progress, so every card starts and ends relative to its own viewport journey.

07 · production constraints

The honest checklist

  • Disable eased scrolling and large positional effects for prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Use one ticker. Calling lenis.raf() from GSAP and another rAF loop creates visible jitter.
  • Refresh measurements after fonts, images and dynamic layout changes.
  • Do not combine virtual scrolling with native CSS scroll timelines unless their positions genuinely remain synchronized.
  • Programmatic scrolling does not move focus; focus meaningful destinations when navigation requires it.
