The gooey merge

The second half of the Liquid Glass language: shapes that fuse as they approach. feGaussianBlur softens the edges, then an feColorMatrix with a steep alpha multiplier (0 0 0 26 -12) snaps everything above the threshold back to opaque — so overlapping blurs become one solid outline.

Costs and caveats

  • backdrop-filter forces the browser to re-rasterise everything behind the element on every frame. Use contain: paint, keep panels small, and never stack them.
  • Safari does not apply SVG filter references in backdrop-filter; the -webkit- line above is the graceful degradation to a plain blur.
  • Contrast is the real risk. Text over a distorting backdrop fails WCAG at some scroll positions — put a solid or near-solid layer behind any text that must be read.
  • Animating baseFrequency on the turbulence gives you flowing liquid, but it re-runs the whole filter chain per frame. Do it on hover only, never ambiently.
