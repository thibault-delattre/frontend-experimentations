When to reach for WebGL instead

  • CSS 3D has no z-buffer. Intersecting geometry sorts by paint order, not by depth — two crossing planes will always show one fully in front of the other.
  • No lighting, no shadows, no textures beyond what CSS can paint.
  • But: it is accessible, selectable, styleable and hardware-composited. For cards, carousels, flips and menus it is the right tool, and it costs nothing to load.
