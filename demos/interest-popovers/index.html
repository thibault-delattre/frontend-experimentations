Interest Invokers & Hints

Interest is broader than hover: pointer dwell, keyboard focus and touch intent can all reveal contextual UI. The browser now has a declarative relationship for it.

Show interest in the link by hovering, focusing, or long-pressing. The preview is supplementary; activating the link remains navigation. A start delay prevents dense prose from erupting with accidental cards.
